# Code of Conduct

Taiwan Market Toolkit aims to be a practical, welcoming open-source project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code, data, or design decisions.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence, tests, and reproducible examples.
- Respect privacy, licensing, and data-source terms.
- Keep financial-market discussions focused on software and data tooling rather than promoting trades or soliciting investment activity.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, spam, or publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these expectations. Repeated or serious violations may result in blocking from the project.

If a concern involves sensitive security information, do not post exploit details in a public issue; follow `SECURITY.md` instead.
